MATERIAL EX BROWSER - DATA RETENTION POLICY

Last updated: 2026-11-21

All data below is stored on your device. The developers do not receive it.

1. Browsing history
Kept for 60 days and then deleted automatically. You can delete single entries or everything at any time, including under Settings > Privacy > Clear browsing data.

2. Search history
Kept for 60 days and then deleted automatically. You can clear it at any time.

3. Bookmarks and shortcuts
Kept until you delete them.

4. Open tabs
Kept so that they can be restored, until you close them.

5. Download list
Kept until you remove it. Removing entries with "Clear browsing data" keeps the downloaded files. Deleting a download from the Downloads screen also deletes its file from the Downloads folder. Downloaded files are stored in the Android Downloads folder, are managed by you and are not deleted automatically.

6. Website permission decisions
Kept until you change or reset them, or clear them under Clear browsing data. Counts of consecutive denials are kept with the decision.

7. Temporary permission grants ("Allow this time")
Kept in memory only, for at most 15 minutes, and removed when you leave the website, close the tab or restart the application.

8. Website data (cookies, site storage, cache)
Kept by the browser engine until the website or you remove it. You can clear cookies and site data, and cached images and files, under Clear browsing data.

9. Settings and legal acceptance records
Kept until you clear the application's storage or uninstall it.

10. Site styles and per-site user agent choices (Android)
The custom CSS you save in the Site editor and the user agent you choose for a website are kept, per website, until you reset them for that website or clear the application's storage. They are not applied in private tabs.

11. Windows application
The Windows version stores its data in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MaterialEX on your computer: settings, bookmarks, the history list (the most recent 5,000 pages, kept until you clear it from the History window), the download list, website permission decisions (including consecutive denial counts), per-website custom CSS and user agent choices, and the browser engine's profile (cookies, site storage and cache). The Windows version has no private browsing mode and no separate profiles. It also keeps two small technical logs (debug.log from the browser engine and lifecycle.log) that record warnings and window events; lifecycle.log is limited to 64 KB. Temporary permission grants are kept in memory only and end when you leave the website or close the tab. Downloaded files are saved to your Windows Downloads folder and are not deleted automatically. Deleting the MaterialEX folder removes all of this data except downloaded files.

12. Removing everything
Clearing the application's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Android application removes all data described here, except files you downloaded to the Android Downloads folder. On Windows, delete the MaterialEX folder as described above.
