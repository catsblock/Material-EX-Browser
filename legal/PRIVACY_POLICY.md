MATERIAL EX BROWSER - PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026-11-20

1. Summary
Material EX does not operate servers that collect your personal data, does not require an account and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code of its own.

2. Data stored on your device
The following data is stored locally in the application's private storage on your device:
- Settings (language, toolbar position, search engine and similar choices).
- Browsing history (page titles, addresses and visit times).
- Bookmarks.
- Open tabs and their navigation state.
- The list of your downloads (file name, source address, size and status) and the downloaded files in your Downloads folder.
- Your decisions about website permissions (camera, microphone, location, notifications and similar) per website.
- Website data kept by the browser engine (cookies, cache, site storage), as with any browser.
- Records of the legal agreements you accepted (version and time of acceptance).
- If you choose to connect a Google account during onboarding, the email address of that account. Authentication is handled by Google Play services on your device. Material EX does not receive your password, does not store access tokens and uses no server of its own. Disconnecting removes the stored email address from the app.
This data is not sent to the developers. Uninstalling the application removes it.

3. Data sent over the network
- Websites: when you visit a website, your device sends the requests needed to load it. The website operator receives standard request data such as your IP address and browser identification. The app opens its own home page, which is shown on your device and does not load a website.
- Search: text you enter in the address field that is not recognised as a web address is sent to the search engine you selected, using that engine's own privacy practices.
- Browser engine: Material EX uses GeckoView, provided by Mozilla. Material EX does not add telemetry to it. Its behaviour is documented by Mozilla.

4. Default browser
If you choose to make Material EX your default browser, the operating system opens links from other apps in Material EX. This is controlled by Android; Material EX does not change the setting silently.

5. Your choices and rights
You can delete history, search history, the download list, website permissions, cookies, site data and cache under Settings > Privacy > Clear browsing data, delete bookmarks individually and remove everything by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling it. Because the developers do not hold your personal data, requests to access, correct or delete it can be fulfilled directly on your device. Where laws such as the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD) apply, you keep the rights they grant you regarding data processed by third parties such as websites and search engines; contact those parties directly.

Windows version: the Windows application also keeps its data only on your computer (see the Data Retention Policy). It supports separate profiles with their own cookies, site data, history, bookmarks and permissions; deleting a profile removes its data the next time the app starts. Private windows keep cookies, cache and site data in memory only and do not record history, but they do not make you anonymous. "Clear browsing data" in its menu deletes history, cookies, cache, the download list and saved site permissions, and you can delete the MaterialEX folder in %LOCALAPPDATA% to remove everything else. Websites you visit, and the search engine you configure, receive the information any browser sends, such as your IP address and, depending on your settings, your user agent.

Extension store (Android): when you open the extension store inside the app, it sends your search words to Mozilla's add-ons service (addons.mozilla.org) to list extensions, and extensions you choose to install are downloaded from Mozilla's servers. Mozilla's own privacy policy applies to that service. The app does not send anything to that service unless you open the store.

6. Children
Material EX is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children.

7. Security
Data is kept in Android's private application storage. No software is completely secure.

8. Changes
Future versions may change this policy. The version in force is the one shipped with the application you are using.
